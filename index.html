About

Evaluating what models can actually do

I am a Machine Learning Researcher at Deccan AI, working on Reinforcement Learning and on evaluation loops for LLMs and agentic systems, across coding and non-coding domains. That means building and certifying RL environments with verifiable rewards, and analyzing large-scale frontier-model rollouts to locate real capability gaps.

I completed my M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Bombay (2023–25) under Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya. My thesis, Large Language Models for Conversation and Evaluation, won the Masters Thesis Excellence Award (2025). Before research, I was an SDE at Amazon, and most recently built an LLM-based AIOps platform at Nexus Cognitive.

ACL 2025 · INDUSTRY

From Recall to Creation: Generating Follow-up Questions using Bloom's Taxonomy and Grice's Maxims

A. Yadav, Harshvivek Kashid, M. Sruthi, B. JayaPrakash, C. R. Kullayappa, M. J. Reddy, et al.
Proceedings of the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL): Industry Track, 2025.
  • B-FQG: follow-up question generation grounded in Bloom's Taxonomy, escalating complexity from recall to creation.
  • GriceWise: reference-free evaluation on Grice's Maxims (Logical Consistency, Informativeness, Relevance, Clarity).
  • Blooms-FQ: human-annotated dataset, 750 seed questions and 3,750 follow-ups; 96.68% human agreement with intended cognitive levels.

HalluDetect: Detecting, Mitigating, and Benchmarking Hallucinations in Conversational Systems in the Legal Domain

S. Anaokar, S. Ganatra, Harshvivek Kashid, S. Bhattacharyya, S. N. Nair, R. Sekhar, et al.
Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing: Industry Track.
