About Me

I am a Machine Learning Researcher at Deccan AI, working on Reinforcement Learning and on evaluation loops for LLMs and agentic systems, across coding and non-coding domains. My work is building and certifying RL environments with verifiable rewards, and analyzing large-scale frontier-model rollouts to locate real capability gaps.

I completed my M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Bombay (2023–25), where I worked with Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya on conversational LLMs, evaluation, and domain-adapted NLP. My thesis, Large Language Models for Conversation and Evaluation, received the Masters Thesis Excellence Award (2025). Before that I was a Software Development Engineer at Amazon, and most recently a Data & AI Engineer at Nexus Cognitive, building an LLM-based AIOps platform.
